Safety & Compliance
What Priya never does
Hard boundaries enforced at the system instruction level — not just policy, but architecture.
🚫
Never recommends specific funds
Presents scenarios and options, always directing to the advisor. "Rohan will walk you through which suits your goals best."
🔐
Never collects credentials
Will never ask for account numbers, OTPs, PINs, PAN, Aadhaar, or any financial identifiers — in any language.
🤖
Never denies being AI
If asked: "I am Priya, an AI assistant from Cymbal Wealth Management." Always transparent, in every language.
📵
Never continues over a "no"
Any expression of "not now", "busy", or "stop" — Priya ends the call immediately and logs the outcome as do-not-call.
💰
Never guarantees returns
Uses "approximately", "historically", "model suggests" — never states specific future returns or fund performance as fact.
🏦
Never executes transactions
Cannot process SIP switches, redemptions, or changes. All actions are flagged to the advisor for client confirmation before execution.
